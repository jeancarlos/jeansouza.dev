# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub: <https://github.com/jeancarlos/jeansouza.dev/security/advisories/new>. You can also email contact@jeansouza.dev. Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

Include the affected page or file, the steps to reproduce, and the impact you expect.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within 7 days.
- A fix or a written assessment within 30 days.
- Coordinated disclosure: please wait for a fix, or 90 days from your report, before publishing details.

## Scope

The site served at jeansouza.dev, its source in `src/` and the CI workflows in `.github/workflows/`. Cloudflare, GitHub Pages and third-party dependencies are out of scope: report those upstream.

## Supported versions

Only the current `main` branch, which is what jeansouza.dev serves.
